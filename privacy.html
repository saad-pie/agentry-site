Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
01 / COLLECTION

1. What we collect

  • Firebase authentication data: email address, display name, and (if Google sign-in) profile photo from your Google account
  • Chat history: messages you send to the chat interface, stored per-user by DeepSeek Harness (DSH)
  • Capability data: capabilities you build, stored in a shared append-only log
  • Technical logs: IP address, user agent, and error events for debugging
02 / EXCLUSIONS

2. What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising trackers, pixels, or third-party analytics
  • No cross-site tracking
  • No browsing history outside this site
  • No payment information (we don't take payments yet)
03 / USAGE

3. How we use data

  • To authenticate you (Firebase)
  • To serve your chat history back to you (DSH)
  • To route capabilities correctly (shared log)
  • To diagnose bugs and prevent abuse (logs)
04 / PROCESSORS

4. Third-party processors

  • Google Firebase (authentication): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Hugging Face (compute hosting): huggingface.co/privacy
  • Vercel (site hosting): vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy
05 / RETENTION

5. Data retention

  • Chat history: retained while your account exists
  • Capability log: retained indefinitely (append-only; individual capabilities can be retired but not deleted from history)
  • Error logs: 30 days
  • Auth tokens: session-based, refreshed on login
06 / RIGHTS

6. Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, deletion of your account and chat history, correction of inaccurate data, or portability of your data.

Email support@try-agentry.vercel.app or saadabdulrehman2010@gmail.com to exercise these rights. Capability log entries are retained for system integrity (the graph is append-only by design), but individual capabilities can be retired on request.

07 / COOKIES

7. Cookies

We use Firebase authentication cookies and DSH session cookies. See /cookies for details. We do not use advertising cookies.

08 / CHILDREN

8. Children

This service is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

09 / CHANGES

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced.

10 / CONTACT

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@try-agentry.vercel.app or saadabdulrehman2010@gmail.com.
